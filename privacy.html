Privacy Policy

CNO Reports · Last updated August 4, 2026

CNO Reports is a private tool CNO Creative Co uses to produce monthly social media performance reports for the clients whose accounts it manages. It reads analytics. It does not post, change, or publish anything.

This policy describes what CNO Reports collects, why, who can see it, and how it is deleted. It covers the CNO Reports application and its connection service only.

Who this applies to

CNO Reports is not a public product. It is used by CNO Creative Co staff, on behalf of client businesses that have engaged CNO to manage their social media presence. The only people whose accounts it connects to are those clients, and only with their explicit authorization.

What is collected

When a client connects an account

A client, or a CNO staff member who already administers the client's account, signs in on the platform's own website (Meta, TikTok, or LinkedIn) and approves read-only analytics access. From that we receive and store:

WhatWhy
An access token, and a refresh token where the platform issues oneTo read that account's analytics on an ongoing basis without asking the client to sign in every month
The names and identifiers of the accounts that sign-in can administerSo CNO can assign the one profile that belongs to that client, and never read another
A client label typed by CNO staffTo keep each client's data in a separate workspace

CNO never receives, asks for, or stores a platform password or two-factor code. Sign-in happens entirely on the platform's own site.

Analytics read from a connected account

  • Account-level figures: reach, impressions, views, profile visits, link clicks, follower counts and changes, and engagement totals.
  • Post-level figures for the account's own posts: reach, impressions, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, and the post's caption text and public link.
  • Where CNO reports on paid media: advertising spend, paid reach, paid impressions, paid clicks, and results, kept separate from organic figures.
  • Aggregate audience breakdowns the platform provides, such as age bands, gender split, and top cities or countries. These are group percentages. They do not identify individual people.
What is never read. CNO Reports does not read direct messages, follower or connection lists, individual audience members' profiles, or any content belonging to anyone other than the connected account itself.

Data CNO staff upload manually

CNO staff can import analytics files exported from the platforms by hand. Those files are read in the staff member's own browser and are not transmitted anywhere unless CNO chooses to create a client report link.

Reports shared with clients

When CNO sends a client their report, the report contents are stored so the link can be opened on any device. That stored copy is encrypted at rest, contains one client's data only, expires automatically after one year, and can be revoked by CNO at any time. CNO can also add a password, in which case the report is encrypted in the browser before it is stored and the password is never sent to the service.

Staff sign-in

CNO staff sign in to the private console with an internal access token. Sessions are stored as an irreversible hash, never as readable credentials.

What is never done

  • No posting, scheduling, commenting, messaging, replying, or modifying anything on any connected account.
  • No selling, renting, or licensing of any data to anyone.
  • No advertising, ad targeting, audience building, or profiling.
  • No combining one client's data with another's. A report contains exactly one client's accounts.
  • No use of client analytics to train machine learning models.

Artificial intelligence

CNO Reports uses an AI language model to write the plain-English summary that accompanies each report. It is sent the aggregate figures for the reporting period so it can describe them. It is not sent access tokens, credentials, or any other client's data. All calculations and charts are produced by the software itself, not by the model, and CNO staff review and edit every sentence before a client sees it.

Who can see the data

  • CNO Creative Co staff, in order to prepare the report.
  • The client the data belongs to, through their own report.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the encrypted data on CNO's behalf.

Nobody else. Data is not disclosed to third parties except where CNO is legally required to do so.

How it is protected

  • Access and refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are written to storage.
  • Tokens are never sent to a web browser and never appear in a report link.
  • Stored report contents are encrypted at rest.
  • All traffic is over HTTPS.
  • Each client is a separate workspace, and a connection cannot be used until CNO has assigned the exact account that belongs to that client.

Your rights

A client may at any time ask CNO to disconnect their accounts, delete the analytics collected from them, revoke a report link, or provide a copy of the data held about their accounts. A client can also revoke CNO's access directly from their own platform settings, which stops all further collection immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Clients with a connected account will be told about any change that affects what is collected or who can see it.

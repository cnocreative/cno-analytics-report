CNO Reports · Last updated August 13, 2026

CNO Reports is a private tool CNO Creative Co uses to produce and deliver monthly social media performance reports for the clients whose accounts it manages. These terms cover use of the tool itself and the connections it makes to a client's social accounts.

Who this is for

CNO Reports is not a public product and is not offered for general sign-up. It is used by CNO Creative Co staff, and by client businesses that have engaged CNO to manage their social media presence, solely to authorize CNO to read that business's own analytics. Connecting an account through CNO Reports must be done by someone who already has administrative access to that account — CNO Reports never grants access to an account beyond what the person connecting it already holds.

What the service does

  • Reads analytics from a client's connected social accounts (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn) that the client or CNO has authorized.
  • Compiles those figures into a written monthly report, reviewed by CNO staff before it reaches a client.
  • Stores an encrypted, client-scoped link so a client can reopen their own report.

CNO Reports never posts, schedules, comments, messages, or otherwise changes anything on a connected account. See the privacy policy for what data is collected and how it is protected.

Acceptable use

CNO Reports may be used only to read analytics for accounts the user is authorized to administer. It may not be used to access another party's account, to circumvent a platform's own terms of service, or to collect data beyond what a report reasonably requires.

Accounts and access

A social account connection can be revoked at any time, either by the client from their own platform settings or by CNO staff from CNO Reports. Revoking access stops all further reading from that account. See the data deletion page for what happens to data already collected.

No warranty

CNO Reports is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. Figures are drawn from each platform's own reporting API and may differ slightly from a platform's native dashboard — differences in timing, attribution windows, or how a platform classifies content are outside CNO's control. CNO reviews figures for large or unexplained discrepancies before a report is sent, but does not warrant that every number is identical to what a platform itself would show.

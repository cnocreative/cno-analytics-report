Deleting your data

CNO Reports · Last updated August 4, 2026

You can disconnect CNO Reports from your social media account at any time, and ask for everything collected from it to be deleted. Here is how, and what happens.

The fastest way: revoke access yourself

You do not need to wait for us. Removing CNO Reports from your own platform settings stops all further collection immediately:

PlatformWhere to remove it
Instagram / FacebookFacebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, then remove CNO Reports
TikTokTikTok Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, then remove CNO Reports
LinkedInLinkedIn Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Other applications → Permitted services, then remove CNO Reports

This revokes our access. It does not by itself delete the analytics we already collected — for that, send us the request below, and we will delete it.

Requesting deletion

  1. Email info@cnocreative.co with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Tell us which account. The business or page name, and which platform (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or LinkedIn). If you manage more than one, say whether you want all of them or specific ones.
  3. Send it from an address we can verify — ideally the one CNO already corresponds with you on. We will confirm you are entitled to make the request before deleting anything, so that nobody else can delete your data.
  4. We confirm when it is done. Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually within a few working days.

What gets deleted

  • The stored authorization for that account, including the encrypted access and refresh tokens.
  • All analytics collected from that account through the connection — account figures, post figures, and audience breakdowns.
  • Any stored client report built from it, so existing report links stop opening.
What this does not reach. Deleting your data from CNO Reports does not change anything on your own social media account. Your posts, followers, and the platform's own analytics are untouched and remain yours. It also does not remove reports CNO has already delivered to you as a saved file, such as a PDF you were sent, because those are in your possession rather than ours.

If you would rather CNO did it for you

If CNO manages your accounts, just ask your CNO contact to disconnect the account. Staff can remove a connection directly, which deletes the stored authorization and the analytics gathered with it in the same action.

Reports already shared with you

Every client report link expires automatically one year after it is created, and CNO can revoke any link before then on request. If you want a specific report link deactivated, say so in the same email and name the report.

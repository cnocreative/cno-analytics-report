Native platform sync

CNO's private connection service collects approved account analytics on a schedule. Platform passwords and OAuth tokens never enter this report or a client share link.

1 · Authorize onceAn account administrator signs in on Meta, TikTok, or LinkedIn and approves read-only analytics access. The platform still controls OAuth and 2FA.
2 · Store securelyThe server encrypts tokens with AES-256-GCM before database storage. CNO staff see connection status, never the token itself.
3 · Refresh automaticallyA scheduled private job refreshes connected accounts. The report receives normalized metrics through a short-lived, single-use import link.
CNO internal console staff only
The service must be deployed and configured once with approved platform apps. After that, connections and refreshes are managed in the private console.
